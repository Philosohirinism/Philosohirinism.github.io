Desire

Do we really desire what we think we desire?

Night Concept Lab

Need, choice, the gaze of others, and the wish to become a desirable self.

About this theme

The film The Substance provides an entry into a discussion of desire. The session distinguishes a practical need from the desire for recognition, visibility, and an imagined, more desirable version of oneself.

Connecting the earlier concepts of alienation and spectacle, the materials trace how images offer models of what is worth wanting, while self-improvement can turn those models into demands. Participants are invited to examine how a particular desire formed and whether it depends on comparison or approval.

Questions for discussion

  • Which desire feels personal but carries the gaze of others?
  • What changes when an ideal self becomes an obligation?
  • Would the same desire remain without comparison or approval?
