Spectacle

Why does life increasingly resemble a display?

Night Concept Lab

Images, visibility, and the shift from living an experience to arranging how it will appear.

About this theme

Using the concept of spectacle, this session asks how social relations become organized through images. The focus is the way anticipation of an audience can shape an experience before it happens.

Social media, consumption, and the presentation of a successful self provide everyday examples. The discussion distinguishes sharing an experience from arranging life around visibility, and asks what room remains for experiences that are not displayed.

Questions for discussion

  • Have you arranged an experience around how it would look?
  • How does an imagined audience change an ordinary day?
  • What would you keep doing without an audience?
