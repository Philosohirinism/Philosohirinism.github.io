Language, Matrices & Social Classification

Academic Bar workshop

An interdisciplinary workshop using mathematical images to examine naming, evaluation, and identity.

About this theme

This workshop brings the presentation Language and Matrices together with the notes on isomorphism and matrix transformations. Row reduction, projection, and eigenvectors provide visual analogies for discussing what a category selects, what an evaluation system makes visible, and what may persist through change.

The examples move from ordinary naming to social labels and expectations. The mathematical images serve as prompts for philosophical discussion: participants compare the complexity of a person with the narrower terms through which a particular setting evaluates them.

Questions for discussion

  • Which part of an experience disappears when it receives a label?
  • How does the same person appear differently under different criteria?
  • What do you recognize as continuous in yourself through change?
