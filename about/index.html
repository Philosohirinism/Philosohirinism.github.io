About

Research, teaching, and ideas.

I’m HAI Weizhou (海维舟). I enrolled at Osaka University’s OSIPP in 2025, where I study International Public Policy.

My academic work concerns critical International Relations, ontological security, ASEAN, sovereignty, and legitimacy.

My work also includes English teaching, admissions support, policy research, and academic communication. Through UjiBricks, I explore ideas in Chinese-language video.
