Introduction

An unscripted reflection on companionship under uncertainty: what can love mean when people need one another but cannot promise an unchanged future?

In this episode

Companionship without certainty

The episode connects fluid life conditions with fatigue, the need for care, and the possibility of leaving.

What are we asking of the future?

Asking what a relationship is can also ask how much of an uncertain future another person is able to commit.

A finite relationship can still matter

A shared train journey becomes an analogy for companionship that has value without an assurance of permanence.
