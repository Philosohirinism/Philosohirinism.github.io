Psychoanalysis & Lacan

If consciousness is not the main character, who am I?

Academic Bar workshop

The unconscious, language, the mirror stage, and the place of others in desire.

About this theme

The session begins with dreams and slips of the tongue, then moves from Freud to Lacan’s account of language and subjectivity. The teaching outline introduces the mirror stage, the symbolic order, and the Other as ways of questioning a self that appears transparent to itself.

Desire, love, and the formation of identity bring these concepts back to familiar experiences. Discussion centres on how language and social expectations participate in what feels like an individual choice, and how that changes the question of who is speaking.

Questions for discussion

  • What do dreams and slips suggest about the limits of conscious intention?
  • How does being recognized shape an image of the self?
  • Which desires carry the expectations of someone else?
