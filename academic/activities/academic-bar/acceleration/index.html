Acceleration

When everything speeds up, where are we going?

Night Concept Lab

Efficiency, time pressure, and the search for a more responsive relationship with the world.

About this theme

This session starts from a familiar puzzle: greater efficiency does not necessarily leave us with more time. It introduces technological acceleration, social change, and the accelerating pace of life through the work of Hartmut Rosa, alongside Paul Virilio’s attention to speed and power.

Examples connect responsiveness at work, visibility, relationships, and self-optimization. Rather than treating slowness as a universal solution, the discussion asks which rhythms allow a meaningful response to the world and how bodily limits can be heard.

Questions for discussion

  • Where does the time saved by technology go?
  • When does responding quickly become a measure of personal worth?
  • Which activities need a rhythm that cannot simply be accelerated?
