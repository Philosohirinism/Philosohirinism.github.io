  1. 0:01 Previously: one person wants to hold on to the past; another wants to strive for the future. In reality, there is only one chair.

  3. 0:16 Episode 3: Those Who Hold On, Those Who Strive
  4. 0:22 Imagine a university street dance club. Its president has led it for three years. The club’s style, performance roster, and ‘who has the final say’ are already settled.
  5. 0:32 A new member joined just six months ago. She wants to dance in her own style and get on the roster for the year-end show.
  6. 0:39 Only the final line of the roster remains ‘to be decided.’
  7. 0:46 This series places them in two positions.
  8. 0:50 What they want — Holding on: for established identities, status, and arrangements of recognition to continue. Striving: a self and a position not yet realized, but believed to be what ought to be.
  9. 0:59 In the club — Holding on: ‘I am the president’; style and roster follow the old rules. Striving: ‘I should be onstage too,’ dancing in my own style.
  10. 1:06 Direction in time — Holding on: toward what already exists. Striving: toward what does not yet exist.
  11. 1:10 The author’s distinction
  12. 1:15 ‘Could we add a piece I choreographed to the year-end show?’
  13. 1:20 For the president, this question makes it uncertain whether the arrangement can stay as it is.
  14. 1:26 ‘The roster is ranked by attendance. That has always been the rule.’
  15. 1:30 For the new member, this answer makes it uncertain whether she will be recognized.
  16. 1:38 The action one side takes for its own security is precisely the uncertainty the other must face.
  17. 1:47 Each side has its own anxiety and its own way of managing it.
  18. 1:52 And their anxieties are connected, driving each other in a recurring loop.
  19. 1:57 Author’s OSIPP draft, 2B(i): ‘Once subjects within the order are differentiated into these two positions, their respective forms of anxiety and the ways they manage it become correlated in a recursive way.’
  20. 2:04 This series’ proposition

  24. 2:26 Pause for a moment: in a relationship in your life right now, are you more like the one holding on or the one striving?
  25. 2:38 Take care: these are two positions, not two kinds of people.

  27. 2:51 One person can even occupy both positions at once: holding on to some things while striving for others.
