The Body

Night Concept Lab

Embodied experience, discipline, labour, images, and technology.

About this theme

Ghost in the Shell opens a discussion of the body as the way a subject encounters the world. The session distinguishes the measured and observed body from the body experienced through pain, fatigue, movement, and sensation.

It connects embodiment to discipline, the exchange of labour power, visibility, desire, and acceleration. Prosthetics, tracking, and digital images return the discussion to a central question: how can we inhabit and care for a body that is continually treated as something to optimize?

Questions for discussion

  • When has your body interrupted a plan or an image of yourself?
  • How do metrics change the way you experience your body?
  • What would it mean to care for the body beyond optimizing it?
