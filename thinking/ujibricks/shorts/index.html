Forty Weeks—Why Eighteen Years?

English introduction

UjiBricks brings political theory into everyday situations through short visual essays in Chinese. The films explore feminism and care, anxiety and recognition, and the relationships between nationhood, statehood, and sovereignty. Each film has an English introduction and selectable English subtitles.
